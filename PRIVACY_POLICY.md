# Privacy Policy — Turbo Drift Racer

**Last updated:** 2026-11-17  
**Developer:** VaelNTN  
**Package ID:** `com.turbodrift.racer`  
**Repository:** https://github.com/VaelNTN/turbo-drift-racer

---

## English (Google Play Store Review)
Turbo Drift Racer is a free arcade drift racing game with no advertisements and no real-money purchases.
- **Local storage:** Selected driver, car, best lap/race times, and player nickname are stored locally on your device (`localStorage`).
- **Online racing & leaderboards (optional):** When racing online or submitting track records, the app transmits only an anonymous generated `deviceId`, chosen nickname, car/driver IDs, race coordinates inside the temporary room, and race times.
- **Data deletion:** Uninstall the app or clear storage to delete local data, or open an issue on GitHub to remove leaderboard records.
